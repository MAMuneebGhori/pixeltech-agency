# Booked Solid System — Website Redesign & Conversion Spec

## 1. Brand & Aesthetic Direction (Anti-AI-Slop Rules)
- **Domain:** Luxury Med-Spa & Aesthetic Clinic AI Automation (PixelTech).
- **Color Palette:** Warm Alabaster background (`#FAF8F5`), Deep Espresso/Obsidian typography (`#141413`), Champagne Gold / Warm Terracotta accents (`#C59B6D`), Sage Green status indicators (`#2E6F40`), Crisp White cards (`#FFFFFF`) with subtle `1px solid #EAE6DF` borders.
- **Typography:** Editorial Serif for headings (`Instrument Serif` or `Playfair Display`), clean geometric Sans-Serif for body/UI (`Plus Jakarta Sans` or `Inter`), and `JetBrains Mono` for latency metrics, timestamps, and live transcript badges.
- **Strict Visual Ban:** NO neon purple/blue AI gradients, NO generic glowing robot brains, NO floating 3D blobs. Every visual must be an interactive UI simulation card (live call transcript, SMS thread, reactivation timeline, or 9 AM owner digest).
